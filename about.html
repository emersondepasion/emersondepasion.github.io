Creative Professional with extensive experience across the Philippines and Cambodia. Started at Workshop One INC. in Manila in 2000, rising from Graphic Artist to Creative Director across a 17-year tenure working on accounts including Coca-Cola, Globe Telecom, San Miguel Corporation, Apple, and Honda Motors.

Relocated to Cambodia in 2018, leading creative for luxury automotive brands including McLaren, Aston Martin, Rolls-Royce, BMW, and Lamborghini, alongside major FMCG and campaign work for Nestlé, Heineken, Tiger Beer, G-Shock, and Grab.

Currently based in Phnom Penh, available for freelance creative direction, art direction, and brand identity work.

Process

AI is used as a research and ideation tool — for concept exploration, reference gathering, and image generation. All creative direction, layout, and execution is done manually. The work is always the result of craft, not automation.
