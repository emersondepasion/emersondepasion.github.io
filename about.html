I began my career at Workshop One Inc. in Manila in 2000, progressing from Graphic Artist to Creative Director during a 17-year tenure. My work covered advertising, branding and campaigns for accounts including Coca-Cola, Globe Telecom, San Miguel Corporation, Apple, and Honda Motors.

After relocating to Cambodia in 2018, I led creative work for luxury automotive brands including McLaren, Aston Martin, Rolls-Royce, BMW, and Lamborghini, alongside campaigns for Nestlé, Heineken, Tiger Beer, G-SHOCK, and Grab.

Now based in Phnom Penh, I work with brands and teams across Asia and international markets. I'm open to senior creative opportunities, remote collaborations and selected freelance projects.

Process

I use AI selectively to support research, concept exploration and image development. Strategy, creative judgment, art direction and final design decisions remain hands-on and led by me.
